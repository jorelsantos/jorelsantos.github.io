2026–Present

Research Discovery Knowledge Graph

An AI-grounded search tool that helps social-science instructors find datasets by how researchers have used them.

Python · Neo4j · semantic search · Next.js · LLM grounding

ContextFinding datasets across ICPSR, OpenAlex, and DataCite is hard: there's no shared discovery layer, and keyword search won't tell you how a dataset has been used. Built at the Institute for Social Research under Prof. Libby Hemphill.

What I didSole builder. I engineered the full stack: a Neo4j knowledge graph (349K nodes, 2M+ relationships) linking datasets to the papers that cite them, semantic search, a graph API, and a Next.js app on Vercel. Mid-build, user testing showed the "evidence-seeker" audience was too broad, so I drove a pivot to a focused instructor segment and rewrote the product brief and 15-week roadmap around it.

ResultsA deployed tool that ranks datasets by citation evidence and shows the citing papers as proof. The pivot narrowed the roadmap to a user we could serve well, after testing exposed where the team's assumptions outran the data.

2026–Present

Vinland

A hybrid-athlete dashboard that unifies lifting and running in one place, logged in plain language instead of forms.

Python · FastAPI · SQLite · pandas · LLM parsing · PWA

ContextEvery fitness tool picks a side: Hevy for lifting, Strava for running. A hybrid athlete training for a half marathon without losing strength has no single view. I wanted one, built around how I log: messy notes, not rigid forms.

What I didSole builder. I built a self-hosted app that parses my Obsidian workout logs into SQLite and serves progression, volume, frequency, and completeness analytics on a FastAPI and pandas backend. An LLM reads a typed or dictated workout, pulls out sets, reps, RPE, and notes (a tweaked shoulder), and writes it back to my notes, which stay the source of truth. Apple Health feeds the running and conditioning side.

ResultsOne screen for the whole training picture, lifts and runs together, that no off-the-shelf app offers, self-hosted at near-zero cost. Lifting analytics and natural-language logging work today; running and dual-progression views land next.

2025–Present

Rabbit Hole

A music-discovery app that maps how artists connect through collaborations. First feature: Six Degrees of Kendrick Lamar, the shortest path from any artist to Kendrick, with a playable preview of the connecting song at every step.

Python · MusicBrainz · graph algorithms · Next.js · Cloudflare Workers/D1 · Spotify API

ContextStreaming platforms recommend by genre and popularity, not by how artists connect through who features on whose tracks. Rabbit Hole fills that gap. Kendrick is the first anchor, and the same engine works for any artist.

What I didSolo builder. I designed and built Rabbit Hole on a $0/month serverless stack (a Cloudflare Worker over D1, a Next.js front end for Vercel), engineering a 119,729-artist collaboration graph from a 30GB MusicBrainz dump to replace a rate-limited Spotify API and precomputing every shortest path to Kendrick with one offline BFS. Every hop shows the artist's photo and a 30-second Spotify preview of the connecting song, and clicking any artist mid-chain opens their own path to Kendrick.

ResultsA working product built on an architecture I validated first: a 67-song preview test auto-resolved 79% and played back 100%, and I gated the 8x graph expansion (14,881 to 119,729 artists) on accuracy and size guardrails before promoting to production.

Center for Academic Innovation — Michigan Online

Rebuilt a capability the team lost when its vendor tool sunset: self-service Salesforce auditing across 350+ objects.

Salesforce (API/SOQL) · SQL · Python · Snowflake

ContextWhen the vendor platform Cuneiform sunset, the Center for Academic Innovation lost the ability to audit how its Salesforce instance was used, right as it weighed a Snowflake migration. Revenue data was also scattered across Coursera, edX, and Salesforce with no shared view.

What I didI built a self-service tool that lets any non-technical CAI team audit Salesforce utilization across 350+ objects, turning a lost capability into a repeatable input for the migration decision. Then I unified the scattered revenue data into one standardized model.

ResultsFinance and leadership got a course-level view of profitability across Michigan Online's 280+ courses and 12M+ learners, and the team could weigh the Snowflake migration on real utilization data, not guesswork.
